Lasagna


Ingredients

  • 9–12 lasagna noodles, uncooked
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • ½ cup shredded Parmesan cheese, divided
  • ½ pound lean ground beef
  • ½ pound Italian sausage
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 36 ounces pasta sauce (or 24 oz marinara)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese or cottage cheese
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Instructions
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook noodles until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  3. Cook beef, sausage, onion, and garlic in a skillet over medium-high heat until browned. Drain any excess fat.
  4. Stir in pasta/marinara sauce, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper. Simmer uncovered for 5–15 minutes to thicken slightly, adjusting seasoning if needed.
  5. In a bowl, mix ricotta/cottage cheese, parmesan, 1½ cups mozzarella, parsley, beaten egg, and a pinch of salt.
  6. Layer in a 9×13-inch baking dish: spread 1 cup meat sauce, arrange noodles, spread ⅓ cheese mixture, then 1 cup meat sauce. Repeat layers twice more, ending with noodles and sauce.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 35–45 minutes. Remove foil, top with remaining mozzarella and parmesan, and bake uncovered for another 10–15 minutes until bubbly and browned.
  8. Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
Tips
  • Authentic Fettuccine Alfredo does not contain cream; the creamy texture comes simply from butter, cheese, and starchy pasta water.
  • Use freshly grated Parmesan (or Parmigiano Reggiano) for the best taste and a smooth, melt-in sauce.
  • Keep the plates warm to prevent the sauce from thickening too quickly when serving.
