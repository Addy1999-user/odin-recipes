Tiramisu


Ingredients
  • 300 g Savoiardi (ladyfingers), about 30 cookies
  • 500 g mascarpone cheese
  • 4 eggs (or 5, depending on recipe variation)
  • 100 g granulated sugar (about ½ cup)
  • 300 ml strong espresso coffee (or robust brewed coffee), cooled
  • 2 tablespoons Marsala wine (optional)
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder (for dusting)
Instructions

  1. Prepare Coffee

    2. Brew strong espresso and let it cool. Mix in Marsala wine if using and set aside.

  2. Separate Eggs

    3. Carefully separate egg whites from yolks. Whip the egg whites with half the sugar until stiff peaks form.

  3. Make Mascarpone Cream

    4. Beat egg yolks with the remaining sugar until light and smooth, then gently fold in the mascarpone until no lumps remain. Carefully fold in the whipped egg whites until the mixture is smooth, airy, and creamy.

  4. Assemble the Layers

    5. Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the coffee for 1–2 seconds. Arrange them in a single layer in your serving dish. Spread half the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers. Repeat with a second layer of dipped ladyfingers, then top with the remaining mascarpone cream.

  5. Dust and Chill

    6. Generously dust the top with unsweetened cocoa powder. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 3–6 hours before serving for best flavor and texture.

  6. Serve

    7. Slice and serve cold, adding extra cocoa or chocolate shavings if desired for decoration.
