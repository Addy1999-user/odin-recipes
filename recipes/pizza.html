Pizza


Ingredients

For the Dough:
  • 4 cups Italian Tipo 00 flour (or bread flour as substitute)
  • 1 1/2 cups cold water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp active dry yeast (or fresh yeast)
For the Sauce:
  • Canned San Marzano tomatoes or whole peeled tomatoes, pureed
  • 2 cloves garlic (optional)
  • Pinch of salt and sugar
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil
For Toppings:
  • Fresh mozzarella (preferably mozzarella di bufala)
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil
Method
  1. Make Dough
    • Mix flour, yeast, water, and salt until just combined.
    • Knead for 15-20 minutes until smooth and elastic.
    • Cover and refrigerate for 24-72 hours for slow fermentation.
    • Before baking, let dough come to room temperature and divide into balls, let rise for 2 hours.
  2. Make Sauce
    • Puree tomatoes with salt, a pinch of sugar, and crushed garlic if desired.
    • Use as is fresh without cooking to maintain brightness.
  3. Preheat Oven
    • Preheat your oven and pizza stone or steel at highest temperature (500-550°F or more), ideally for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
  4. Shape Dough
    • Stretch dough balls gently by hand to about 12 inches diameter.
    • Place on a pizza peel lightly dusted with flour or semolina.
  5. Assemble Pizza
    • Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over the dough.
    • Add slices or chunks of fresh mozzarella.
    • Drizzle with olive oil and add fresh basil leaves.
  6. Bake Pizza
    • Slide pizza onto hot stone and bake 4-8 minutes until crust is charred in spots and cheese is melted and bubbly.
  7. Serve
    • Drizzle with more olive oil and fresh basil before serving.
